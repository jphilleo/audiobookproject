Support Audiobook Project

Every free audiobook is narrated on our servers. Donations keep them running, so the free voices can stay free for everyone.

{{donationCallout}}

Where the money goes

  • Servers that narrate books. A full novel takes about three hours of computer time to narrate.
  • Storage and bandwidth for the audio you download and stream.
  • Development: better chapter detection, more voices and languages, and fixes for books that come out wrong.

Good to know

  • Audiobook Project isn't a registered nonprofit, so donations aren't tax-deductible.
  • Donating doesn't unlock anything. The free tool is the same for everyone.
  • Can't donate? Reporting a book that came out wrong on GitHub helps just as much.
