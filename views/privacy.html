Privacy

The short version: your books are only used to make your audiobook, only you can see them, and they're deleted after 30 days.

Last updated November 2026

What we store

  • The book you upload, converted to plain text, plus its title, author and cover.
  • The audio we make from it, and which voice and chapters you chose.
  • An essential cookie containing a random ID, so your browser can find its own books. It is not used for website analytics.

Your browser also remembers your last voice and where you stopped listening. That stays on your device and is never sent to us.

What we don't do

  • The converter does not require an account or email. Optional library download requests require an email address.
  • We don't use your books to train AI models.
  • We don't show ads or sell your data. Our website analytics does not set a tracking cookie.
  • We don't send your books to other companies. The free voices run on our own servers.

How long we keep it

Each book, its text and its audio are deleted automatically 30 days after upload. The book page shows the exact date. You can delete a book sooner with "Delete this book", which removes everything immediately.

Who can see it

Only the browser that uploaded the book can see it in the library or change it. Download and podcast-feed links contain a long random code. Anyone you give one of those links to can listen, so share them only with people you trust.

Library download requests

If you request a library audiobook by email, we store your email address, chosen book and format, confirmation status, and delivery status. Resend processes the recipient address and transactional message to deliver confirmation and download emails. We do not subscribe you to marketing emails. The confirmation link expires after 48 hours.

Confirmed requests are retained while the book is being prepared, for up to 90 days. After the download email is accepted by our email provider, the request and email address are deleted after 30 days. You can remove your request sooner from its private request page. We keep a salted hash of the requesting network address for up to 24 hours to limit abuse. Public-domain audiobook files may remain available for other readers after your request is removed.

Premium voices

When premium voices launch, the text of chapters you choose to narrate with them will be sent to that voice provider to generate the audio. We'll name the provider and link its privacy policy before you choose a premium voice. Free voices never leave our servers.

Website analytics

We count page views, public audiobook plays, download clicks, podcast-app clicks, successful requests and confirmations, and converter activity. We group traffic into broad source categories and mobile or desktop screen sizes.

To estimate daily unique visitors and listening and download-click rates, our server briefly uses the network address and browser User-Agent that accompany a normal web request. It creates a keyed hash with a random key that changes each UTC day. The previous key is discarded. Analytics does not save the raw address or User-Agent, and does not link visitors across days. Network changes, shared networks and similar browsers can affect these estimates.

For each daily hash we retain only whether it visited, listened or clicked a download—not its pages, books, email, payment details or uploaded content. Analytics is hosted on our own server. We do not create a cookie, local-storage identifier or session recording for analytics. Any visitor cookie from our earlier optional system is removed when you next visit; previous declines remain honored until their saved preference expires.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable analytics collection. Private link tokens, query strings, emails and uploaded book details are excluded. Daily stage flags and aggregate counts expire within 90 days. Network addresses used for endpoint abuse limits are held briefly in memory and cleared every minute. Website server logs are separate, as described below.

Server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security, for up to 30 days.

Questions

Ask on GitHub. Because the code is open source, you can also read exactly how files are handled.
