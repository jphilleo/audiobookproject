Exploration, round 6

The humpback is settled. This round picked Public Sans for the wordmark and page titles (Literata was tried first, but the serif didn’t feel right), and the "Free to all" seal; both are now in the brand guide. The other options stay here for comparison.

Typeface

Each card shows the same five things: the site header at real size, the full lockup, a page title over body text in the system font, the lockup on ink, and a seal. Only the wordmark and page titles change; buttons, labels and body copy stay in the system font, so the site stays fast and native. All six are free (SIL Open Font License) and can be self-hosted.

Seals

The seal is a badge, not a second logo: for stickers, the donate page, social avatars and printed things. The humpback sits in the middle and the name runs around the top. Set in Literata.
